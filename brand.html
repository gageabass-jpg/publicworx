Don't

Don't recolor or rearrange the grid blocks — the tile is fixed artwork.
Don't place the tile on a light ground — it lives on the dark ramps.
Don't bolden the wordmark past 500 or set it in another face.
Don't drop the TMH Radiology line in first-touch contexts (sign-in, public header).
